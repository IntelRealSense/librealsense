Detailed Description

Cast-target struct for RS2_COMPOSITE_OPTION_HDRD_CONTROL (see rs_composite_option.h). Wire layout: dpp_header + 8 int32 param slots, 7 used (38 bytes, little-endian, pack(1)); ctl_id = 0x0008. Open item: activates at 720p/960p only - resolution-gating not yet encoded here.
